About George Brown:

George Brown offers more than 200 full-time programs across art and design, business, 
community services, early childhood education, construction and engineering 
technologies, health sciences, hospitality and culinary arts, preparatory studies, 
as well as specialized programs and services for recent immigrants and 
international students

George Brown College has 3 campuses in Toronto

George Brown College have 3 campuses: Casa Loma Campus, St. James Campus, Waterfront campus

Casa Loma Campus is located at 160 Kendal Avenue

St. James Campus is located at 200 King Street East

Waterfront Campus is located at 51 Dockside Dr
