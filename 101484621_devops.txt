<== Developer Operations ==>

COMP 3104 - DevOps is a technical course focused on teaching how to 
use modern tools like Travis CI and GitHub, and Docker to effectivley manage 
continuous integration (CI) and continuous deployment (CD) within large projects.

DevOps helps developers and operations teams by promoting automation, 
collaboration, and reliablity throught the SDLC. The course emphasizes 
building efficient pipelines, maintaining version control, and automating 
testing and deployment.
