=== George Brown College ===

George brown college is a public college that offers a career focused post-secondary
programs in toronto. It's a polytechnic institution that provides real life training to
the industry to students. While the institution is located in Toronto,
it has three campuses: St James, Casa Loma, and Water Front campus.

The institution promotes Hands-on learning including experientail learning, labs, and field placement.
It also provides support to international students which enables them to be flexible in learning, depending
if they are continuing education, full-time undergraduate, or part-time. Lastly, they provide wide range of
full-time programs to cater students' needs like credentials ranging from certificates to diplomas.
