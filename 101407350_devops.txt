=== DEVOPS ===

Devops is a practice of collaborative work between the organization's
software development and the IT operations teams. Devops is a philosophy promoting
better communication between different teams.

The DEVOPS has multiple common methodologies being used like the CI/CD, System/tools,
and implementation of cloud computing, microservices, and containers. CI/CD is a the tools
emphasizing task automation. Also, System/tools are the real-time monitoring, management of configuration,
management of incidents, etc.
