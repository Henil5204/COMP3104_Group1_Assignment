<== George Brown College ==>

Located in the heart of Toronto, George Brown College (GBC) offers a variety 
of courses in business, technology, health sciences, hospitality, design, and 
many other exciting fields. The college focuses on hands-on learning and helping 
students build real skills they can use in their careers. It also provides opportunities 
like the Capstone Project course to work and gain experience that prepares students for 
the workplace.
