<== SDLC ==>

DevOps plays an important role in the software development life cycle (SDLC). 
Traditional SDLC modles sepparate development and operations, leading to 
delays, miscommunication, and a higher risk of errors. DevOps promotes 
collaboration between these two phases of a project through a variety of 
tools and a DevOps mindset.

By integrating testing, deployment, and monitoring into every stage of the 
SDLC, DevOps helps teams deliver software faster, more reliably, and with 
fewer issues after release.
